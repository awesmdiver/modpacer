Settings

Downloads folder ?
Where your downloaded updates go

Pick your mod manager's downloads folder so it sees them right away:

Vortex
Settings → Download → Download Folder
Mod Organizer 2
Settings → Paths → Downloads

Or pick any folder you like if you'd rather add them to your mod manager by hand.

Your Mod Organizer 2 folder (the one with ModOrganizer.exe)
Your Skyrim folder

Your SkyrimNet folder (only if ModPacer can't find it)
Mod Staging Folder ?
Where your installed mods live

Choose the folder where your mod manager keeps your installed mod files:

Vortex
Look in Settings → Mods → Mod Staging Folder Default: C:\Users\<You>\AppData\Roaming\Vortex\skyrimse\mods or a custom drive folder
Mod Organizer 2 (MO2)
Look in Settings → Paths → Mods Default: inside your MO2 portable folder under mods, or %LOCALAPPDATA%\ModOrganizer\<Instance>\mods

Note: Don't choose your game's own folder or the Data folder. Pick the folder your mod manager uses instead.

Nexus API key (Premium members)

If you have Nexus Premium, mods hosted on Nexus can download automatically too. Find your key on Nexus Mods > Account > Site preferences > API Keys. Scroll to the bottom to view your key. Without Premium, you'll get a link to the mod's Nexus page instead.
